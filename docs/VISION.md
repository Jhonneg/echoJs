# Product vision

ECHO is a universal visualizer, not a streaming service or an ordinary music player. It turns sound into immersive, art-directed, responsive worlds. The user listens through their preferred authorized audio source; ECHO is a companion. “Universal” is the vision, not a guarantee of arbitrary system audio, platforms or browsers.

## Experience
Open ECHO → select a supported audio source (shared browser tab or local file) → explicitly authorize capture, if needed → enter a living landscape → adjust reactivity/quality, enter fullscreen or disconnect. Local files are played within ECHO; shared tabs retain their original controls. No account is required. Show clear connected, waiting/silent, unsupported, denied, ended and error states; no implication of direct streaming integration.

## POC (current)
Prove audio capture and lifecycle first on desktop Chrome (Linux reference environment), using a permitted test page/audio. Validate local file as a second input. Then one hybrid scene, Bloom, with real audio signals, ambient motion and basic diagnostics. No polished UI, additional worlds, backend, authentication, AI, beat detection, recording, system audio guarantees or streaming SDKs. Do not spend on art refinement until audio gate passes or the owner explicitly changes direction.

## MVP after POC approval (proposed, not implementation permission)
Tab audio and local files; immersive Bloom scene, fullscreen, reactivity and graphics quality controls, clear source connection/disconnection and graceful errors. Microphone is optional later. Additional landscapes, browser extension/system capture, catalog, accounts and sharing remain future ideas, not commitments. Obtain explicit approval before widening scope.

## Design pillars
Atmosphere over realism; meaningful audio response over decoration; distinct compositions over recolored copies; simple geometry and selective effects; privacy by design; accessible controls; performance and token efficiency. A junior-feasible project and a reusable AI-assisted development methodology are co-equal learning goals.

## Success
A visitor can use a supported, permitted audio source and perceive a coherent scene reacting to it without interrupting playback. The owner can resume in a fresh Codex session from versioned context with little manual prompt writing. No guaranteed token budget or commercial music compatibility is asserted.
